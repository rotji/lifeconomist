# Current Project State

**Last updated:** 2026-10-21
**Stage:** Foundational framework selected; measurement protocol incomplete.

## Established
- The project's research and development framework consists of Workstream A (Deep Conceptual Foundations), Workstream B (Measurement), Workstream C (Evidence-Based Monitoring), and Workstream D (Footprint Intelligence); the definitions, boundaries, and Reality-First Principle are in `RESEARCH-METHODOLOGY.md`.
- The project is global in scope, with Nigeria as an initial test case rather than a permanent limit.
- The agreed four-layer working framework is documented in `README.md`: core indicators, supporting measurements, mechanisms and transmission, and evidence-based state assessment.
- Seven candidate states and six foundational indicators are listed in `README.md`.
- Monitoring may begin as an evidence register, but formal state declarations and transition rules must wait for measurement and historical testing.
- Foundational concepts can be revisited when needed.

## Workstream position
- **A — Deep Conceptual Foundations:** Being established as the home for deep conceptual investigation. The first planned subject is production, beginning with everyday examples; this investigation is not yet reported as complete.
- **B — Measurement:** Actively developing the draft specification for Indicator 1, Real GDP Growth. Its measurement questions remain unresolved as recorded in `03-measurement/01-real-gdp-growth.md`.
- **C — Evidence-Based Monitoring:** Designated for evidence-based monitoring and investigation. A complete monitoring system and validated state-classification process are not established.
- **D — Footprint Intelligence:** Formally designated as a reusable, cross-domain investigation capability. The detailed architecture and biological-related extensions remain proposed; no comprehensive or validated footprint-intelligence system is established.
- The workstreams are expected to evolve together through feedback; none is a prerequisite for all work in another.
