# Current Project State

**Last updated:** 2026-10-27  
**Stage:** Foundational framework selected; measurement protocol incomplete; Workstream E now has an initial FX investigation programme specification.

## Established
- The project's research and development framework consists of Workstreams A (Deep Conceptual Foundations), B (Measurement), C (Evidence-Based Monitoring), D (Footprint Intelligence), and E (Opportunity Intelligence and Economic Application); the definitions and boundaries are in `RESEARCH-METHODOLOGY.md`.
- The project is global in scope, with Nigeria as an initial test case rather than a permanent limit.
- The agreed four-layer working framework is documented in `README.md`: core indicators, supporting measurements, mechanisms and transmission, and evidence-based state assessment.
- Seven candidate states and six foundational indicators are listed in `README.md`.
- Monitoring may begin as an evidence register, but formal state declarations and transition rules must wait for measurement and historical testing.
- Foundational concepts can be revisited when needed.

## Workstream position
- **A — Deep Conceptual Foundations:** The concept-note directory is established at `workstream-a/`. The first concept, production, is documented in `workstream-a/concepts/production.md` as a living explanation; the investigation remains in progress, with questions for deeper review recorded in the note.
- **B — Measurement:** Actively developing the draft specification for Indicator 1, Real GDP Growth. Its measurement questions remain unresolved as recorded in `03-measurement/01-real-gdp-growth.md`.
- **C — Evidence-Based Monitoring:** Designated for evidence-based monitoring and investigation. A complete monitoring system and validated state-classification process are not established.
- **D — Footprint Intelligence:** Formally designated as a reusable, cross-domain investigation capability. The detailed architecture and biological-related extensions remain proposed; no comprehensive or validated footprint-intelligence system is established.
- **E — Opportunity Intelligence and Economic Application:** Established as a working direction to discover, investigate, evaluate, and test potential trading, investment, business, and intelligence-service opportunities. The charter is documented in `WORKSTREAM-E-ARCHITECTURE.md`. The FX investigation plan is now documented in `workstream-e/fx/FX-OPPORTUNITY-INTELLIGENCE-PROGRAMME.md`; its hypotheses, data methods, and potential opportunities remain untested.

## Not yet established

- Preferred data series and source hierarchy for each indicator.
- Observation frequency and historical comparison windows.
- Rules for revisions, missing values, inflation, currency effects, and cross-country comparability.
- Evidence-based transition criteria.
- A validated FX trading strategy, executable FX arbitrage opportunity, or FX intelligence business.

## Immediate working position
Workstream A is continuing Concept 1, **production**, using `workstream-a/concepts/production.md` as the durable reference. The current investigation distinguishes production and usefulness from GDP's production boundary, prices, costs, value added, profit, services, and productivity. Continue expanding the note when understanding advances; do not treat it as a finished national-accounts specification. The draft GDP measurement specification remains open in Workstream B and is not the immediate conversational task.

Workstreams A and E will be explored concurrently when useful: while investigating a substantial concept in A, also consider whether it suggests a practical E opportunity worth testing. E may also start an investigation and send questions back to A, B, C, or D. Use the starting point that best fits the question; do not force a commercial angle where none is justified. Record actual E outcomes—including gains, losses, criticism, and surprises—and feed justified lessons back into the relevant workstreams. Begin with small, evidence-led, affordable tests rather than building an opportunity platform prematurely.

### Workstream E — FX programme
The initial FX programme investigates mathematical currency relationships, economic connections, information timing, market access and conversion friction, fully funded currency rotation, broker-based FX products as a separate track, and potential FX intelligence products/services. Global scope and Nigeria-specific access questions are both in scope. Historical/public data, manual calculations, spreadsheets and simulation come before live trading. The available $20 is not to be risked as part of the initial research. See `workstream-e/fx/FX-OPPORTUNITY-INTELLIGENCE-PROGRAMME.md` for the living specification and first practical task.
