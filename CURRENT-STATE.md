## Established
- The project's research and development framework consists of Workstreams A (Deep Conceptual Foundations), B (Measurement), C (Evidence-Based Monitoring), D (Footprint Intelligence), and E (Opportunity Intelligence and Economic Application); the definitions and boundaries are in `RESEARCH-METHODOLOGY.md`.
- The shared Reality-First Principle now explicitly includes interconnected-reality and layered-investigation guidance. This is a cross-cutting method, not a new workstream. The specialized D process is documented in `FOOTPRINT-INTELLIGENCE-ARCHITECTURE.md`; its architecture remains proposed rather than implemented or validated.
- The project is global in scope, with Nigeria as an initial test case rather than a permanent limit.
- A provisional [Global Financial System Exploration Map](GLOBAL-FINANCIAL-SYSTEM-EXPLORATION-MAP.md) now organizes broad candidate domains, questions, measurement views, source families, accounting safeguards, and possible applications. It is an exploration plan, not a completed dataset or a new core-indicator set.
- The agreed four-layer working framework is documented in `README.md`: core indicators, supporting measurements, mechanisms and transmission, and evidence-based state assessment.
- Seven candidate states and six foundational indicators are listed in `README.md`.
- Monitoring may begin as an evidence register, but formal state declarations and transition rules must wait for measurement and historical testing.
- Foundational concepts can be revisited when needed.

## ECAI — Economic Consequence and Agency Intelligence

- **Status:** Provisional research framework documented in `ECAI-FOUNDATION.md`; not validated as a unified method, product, or intervention system.
- **Purpose:** Connect analysis of economic consequences with evidence-based assessment of what individuals and groups can realistically influence.
- **Architecture:** Cross-cutting application of existing Workstreams A–E and the Reality-First methodology, not a new workstream and not a replacement for the Global Financial System Exploration Map.
- **Current stage:** Stage 1 — foundational literature synthesis in progress. [ECAI-LITERATURE-SYNTHESIS-001.md](ECAI-LITERATURE-SYNTHESIS-001.md) records the initial source-backed synthesis across consumer welfare, political consumerism, boycotts, collective action, production networks, and evaluation. This is a selective initial synthesis, not a systematic review.
- **Not established:** A complete literature review, validated consequence-tracing protocol, comparative case register, or preferred practical application.
- **Initial synthesis findings:** Keep participation, market effects, institutional response, social outcomes, and durability separate; distinguish boycotts from buycotts; match supply-chain claims to data resolution; assess unintended and distributional effects; treat collective-action principles as context-dependent.
- **Next action:** Expand and verify the literature, then specify a repeatable consequence-tracing methodology and examine documented cases before selecting an implementation direction.

## Immediate working position

Workstream A has documented concept notes through **Concept 32 — Commonly Confused Economic and Financial Terms: Comparison Register** ([concept note](workstream-a/concepts/commonly-confused-economic-and-financial-terms-comparison-register.md)). The register compares consequential distinctions across money, wealth, capital, saving, investment, revenue, profit, cash flow, output, productivity, GDP, debt, deficits, liquidity, solvency, funding, financing, financial markets, public finance, ownership, distribution and evidence. It is a cross-reference layer, not a replacement for detailed concept notes or context-specific accounting, legal and statistical standards. Concepts 29–31 cover measurement and inference, time and transmission, and distribution of economic power and opportunity.

The draft GDP measurement specification remains open in Workstream B and is not the immediate conversational task.

Workstreams A and E will be explored concurrently when useful: while investigating a substantial concept in A, also consider whether it suggests a practical E opportunity worth testing. E may also start an investigation and send questions back to A, B, C, or D. Use the starting point that best fits the question; do not force a commercial angle where none is justified. Record actual E outcomes—including gains, losses, criticism, and surprises—and feed justified lessons back into the relevant workstreams. Begin with small, evidence-led, affordable tests rather than building an opportunity platform prematurely.

### Workstream E — FX programme
The initial FX programme investigates mathematical currency relationships, economic connections, information timing, market access and conversion friction, fully funded currency rotation, broker-based FX products as a separate track, and potential FX intelligence products/services. Global scope and Nigeria-specific access questions are both in scope. Historical/public data, manual calculations, spreadsheets and simulation come before live trading. The available $20 is not to be risked as part of the initial research. See `workstream-e/fx/FX-OPPORTUNITY-INTELLIGENCE-PROGRAMME.md` for the living specification and first practical task.
