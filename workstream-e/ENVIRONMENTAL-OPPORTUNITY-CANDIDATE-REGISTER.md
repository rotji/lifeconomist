## Status definitions

- **Longlisted; not screened:** candidate emerged from the broad territory map but has not yet been compared against evidence.
- **Screening:** structured evidence gathering is underway.
- **Advance:** sufficient evidence supports a defined next investigation or test.
- **Hold:** important but blocked by a named dependency or evidence gap.
- **Merge:** combine with a related candidate while retaining provenance.
- **Reject for now:** evidence or constraints make further work unjustified at present.
- **Monitor:** wait for a specified event or change.
- **Validated for defined scope:** the evidence required for a particular use case is adequate; this is not universal validation.
