# 2025 SNA Application and Retrieval Index

**Status:** Initial chapter-level navigation index established; paragraph-level mapping remains to be developed as needed.  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-23  
**Purpose:** Preserve the 2025 SNA as a reusable reference for Lifeconomist without requiring the user to complete or memorize a sequential textbook course.

## 1. How to use this index

This is a navigation aid, not a substitute for the official 2025 SNA. It helps Lifeconomist recognize which chapter may be relevant to a practical question. When a topic is triggered:

1. Identify the real-world question or decision.
2. Search this index for likely chapters and related terms.
3. Open the exact chapter and paragraph(s) in the official 2025 SNA.
4. Check whether a lesson has already been recorded in the study checkpoint.
5. Explain only the concepts needed for the current task.
6. Apply the rule to the evidence/data, noting assumptions and limitations.
7. Record meaningful new learning and add a better retrieval cue if needed.

**Do not infer paragraph numbers from chapter titles.** The chapter-level descriptions below are navigation hints. Exact paragraph references must be checked in the official source before use.

## 4. Where the chapters sit in the wider framework

| Part | Chapters | Navigation purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| A. Introduction and overview | 1–3 | Understand the purpose, scope and integrated architecture. |
| B. The main foundations | 4–6 | Understand the accounting rules, economic units, sectors, residence and production units. |
| C. Structure of the framework and sequence of economic accounts | 7–21 | Measure production, income, transfers, consumption, saving, investment, finance, assets, labour, prices and productivity; integrate and communicate the accounts. |
| D. Cross-cutting issues | 22–27 | Retrieve guidance on digitalisation, globalization, insurance, financial instruments, Islamic finance and contractual rights. |
| E. Institutional units and sectors in more detail | 28–33 | Analyse corporations, financial institutions, government, non-profits, households and cross-border positions. |
| F. Extended and thematic accounts and tables | 34–39 | Extend analysis to well-being, sustainability, input-output relationships, financial counterparties, thematic accounts and informality. |

## 6. Retrieval status vocabulary

Use these labels when the index is expanded:

- **Chapter mapped:** chapter title and broad retrieval cues recorded.
- **Paragraph mapped:** exact paragraph numbers and topics verified against the official White Cover.
- **Introduced:** discussed at an introductory level; not a complete technical study.
- **Studied in depth:** examined in sufficient detail for the stated purpose, with source verification.
- **Applied:** used in a real Lifeconomist task, with the use case and evidence recorded.
- **Open / verify:** relevant question remains unresolved or needs further source checking.

Do not label all 39 chapters as studied. At this stage, the index is a chapter-level map; only the checkpointed lessons have been taught.

## 7. Maintenance rules

- Preserve the official chapter numbering and titles.
- Keep chapter summaries short; detailed explanations belong in lesson records or applied case files.
- Add exact paragraph mappings only after checking the official 2025 SNA White Cover.
- When a real task reveals a missing retrieval cue, update this index.
- If the official standard is revised or a newer authoritative version is issued, record the version change and assess its effect before replacing references.
- Distinguish SNA provisions from BPM7, GFS, MFS, SEEA, ILO and other related standards; cross-reference rather than silently merge them.
- Avoid duplication: one canonical lesson record, one retrieval index entry, and one practical application record where necessary.
