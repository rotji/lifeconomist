# Indicator 1 — Real GDP Growth

**Status:** Draft specification; not yet approved for classification.

## Concept and purpose
Measure the change in the volume of economic output after accounting for changes in prices.

## Operational definition
To be finalized for the selected economy and data source. Decide whether to use annual growth, year-over-year quarterly growth, quarter-over-quarter seasonally adjusted growth, or more than one view. Do not mix these measures without labeling them.

## Formula
For comparable real GDP values in periods t and t-1:

`Growth (%) = (Real GDP_t / Real GDP_(t-1) - 1) × 100`

The comparison period must be explicit.

## Required metadata
- Economy and geographic coverage
- Statistical agency and series name
- Observation period and publication date
- Unit and reference/base year, if applicable
- Seasonal adjustment
- Revisions or methodology changes

## Interpretation cautions
- Real GDP growth is not the same as household welfare, productivity growth, or income distribution.
- Revisions can materially change the historical picture.
- Different quarterly and annual growth measures can tell different stories.
- One observation should not determine an economic-state classification.

## Open questions

- Which frequency best serves the model?
- What historical window is available and methodologically consistent?
- Which official source and series should be preferred?
