# Economic State Transition Model

## Purpose
Develop an evidence-based framework for observing economic conditions, explaining transmission mechanisms, and testing transitions between economic states.

## Current position
The seven candidate states and six foundational indicators have been selected as a working framework. The indicators still need operational definitions, data sources, frequencies, adjustment methods, and limitations. Do not classify the current economy or invent transition thresholds yet.

## Candidate economic states
1. Productivity-led expansion
2. Credit-fuelled expansion
3. Late-cycle overheating
4. Monetary tightening and slowdown
5. Recession and credit contraction
6. Recovery and reflation
7. Long-term debt stress and deleveraging

Long-term debt stress is structural and may overlap cyclical states. The states are not a fixed sequence.

## Six foundational indicators
1. Real GDP growth
2. Labor productivity growth
3. Private-sector credit growth
4. Private-sector credit relative to GDP
5. Debt-service burden
6. Productive investment

## Working principles
- Separate facts, interpretations, hypotheses, and unresolved questions.
- Record source, publication date, observation period, units, and limitations.
- Distinguish stocks from flows and nominal from real measures.
- Treat plausible transmission pathways as hypotheses until supported by evidence.
- Preserve important decisions and changes with reasons.
- Observe first; classify states only after definitions and historical tests are adequate.

## Experimental tool roles
- ChatGPT: explore questions, challenge explanations, draft and review research.
- VS Code: edit Markdown, inspect data, and later develop reproducible analysis.
- Obsidian: maintain connected concept and research notes.
- GitHub: version history and authoritative project record.
- Zotero: manage papers, reports, and bibliographic references.
- LibreOffice Calc / Google Sheets: test indicator registers and data tables.

These tools are not automatically integrated. Important findings and decisions must be deliberately recorded.

## First milestone
Establish durable project records, then complete the measurement protocol for all six indicators. Begin only as an observation/evidence register—not an authoritative current-state classification.
