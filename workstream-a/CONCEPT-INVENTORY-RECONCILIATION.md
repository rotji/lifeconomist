# Workstream A — Concept Inventory Reconciliation

**Date:** 2026-10-30  
**Checkpoint reviewed:** Concepts 1–32  
**Purpose:** Reconcile the 25 planned terminology groups against the existing concept notes before selecting Concept 33. This is an inventory and scope audit, not a new economic concept note or a claim that every note has passed full technical validation.

## 1. Executive result

The 25 planned groups all have at least one identifiable primary concept note or comparison-register entry. The audit did **not** find a whole group that clearly lacks any concept-level treatment. That does not mean every subtopic is fully exhausted, every definition is universally applicable, or every note is empirically validated. The notes are living explanations; formal indicators, official data choices and jurisdiction-specific calculations remain separate tasks.

Two concrete index defects were identified and corrected during this reconciliation:

1. **Concept 6 — Value Added, Income Distribution and Profit** existed in the repository but was omitted from the main Workstream A concept index.
2. **Concept 25 — Capital Markets, Institutional Investors and Investment Funds** appeared twice in that index.

The audit also makes explicit that the 25-group inventory is a thematic map, not a strict numbering scheme. Group 15 maps to Concept 23, Group 16 to Concept 22, and subsequent concept numbering follows the actual investigation sequence and dependencies.

## 4. Intentional overlaps to preserve

These overlaps are useful when boundaries are clear; they should not be removed simply to make every topic appear only once.

- **Production, value added, profit and GDP:** Concepts 1–6 and 13 share production relationships but use different accounting questions and boundaries.
- **Productivity, labour and growth:** Concepts 7, 14 and 26 examine productivity at different levels—production inputs, labour/households and aggregate development.
- **Money, credit, markets and liquidity:** Concepts 9, 10, 18, 21 and 24 connect through balance sheets and payment/funding mechanisms but describe different objects and constraints.
- **Investment, financial markets and funds:** Concepts 11, 18 and 25 distinguish capital allocation, market infrastructure and fund/investor roles.
- **Institutions, structural change and distribution:** Concepts 23, 26 and 31 connect arrangements to changing economic structure and the allocation of benefits, power and risks.
- **Trade and value chains:** Concepts 2, 6, 13 and 22 connect intermediate inputs, value added, GDP and multi-stage commercial networks.
- **Measurement and time:** Concepts 29 and 30 supply shared evidence and temporal safeguards across all substantive concepts.
- **Concept 32 comparison register:** It intentionally repeats terminology in compact form so researchers can navigate to the detailed note; it should not grow into a duplicate encyclopedia.

## 5. Remaining work: distinguish conceptual gaps from implementation work

The audit does not justify creating a new Concept 33 solely because the next number is available. The next review should be a **depth and dependency audit**, not another broad concept note by default.

### A. Validate coverage within existing notes
- Compare each roadmap bullet against the body of its linked note, not only its title or summary.
- Mark each bullet as **explicitly covered**, **partially covered**, **cross-linked**, or **not yet covered**.
- Record concrete missing explanations or examples, not vague requests for “more depth.”
- Add important omissions to the existing note when they fit its scope.

### B. Keep formal measurement separate
- Workstream B must still define the exact indicator, numerator/denominator, unit, geography, population/entity boundary, frequency, source, revisions and validation checks.
- Nigeria-specific series and calculations remain unverified until primary sources and a reproducible measurement protocol are established.
- Do not label a concept as empirically validated merely because the explanatory note is complete.

### C. Select a new concept only if warranted
Create Concept 33 only if the audit identifies a coherent, consequential topic that:
1. is not already adequately explained in an existing note;
2. cannot be addressed more cleanly by adding a section to an existing note;
3. has clear relationships to prior concepts and one or more downstream workstreams; and
4. can be investigated without inventing a false claim of completeness.

### 9.5 Recommended next decision

Choose a specific measurement or monitoring question before selecting the next task, but treat this as a decision aid rather than a compulsory next step. A Workstream B task should define the indicator, construct, unit, denominator, population, geography, period, source, transformation, revision treatment and validation method. A Workstream C task should specify the event/state to detect, evidence sources, decision-time availability, confidence rules, false-alarm/missed-event costs and escalation process.

Workstreams B and C are both incomplete; neither has to be completed before the project moves elsewhere. Select the next task based on clear decision value, feasible evidence, and current priorities—not by creating Concept 33 by default.
