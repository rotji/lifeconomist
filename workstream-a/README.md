# Workstream A — Conceptual Foundations

This directory preserves the detailed explanations developed while investigating concepts under **Workstream A — Deep Conceptual Foundations**.

The goal is not merely to store short definitions. Each concept note should connect terminology to real-world activity, explain mechanisms and relationships, distinguish what is known from what is illustrative or unresolved, and record connections to measurement and practical application.

## How to use this directory

Create one durable note for each substantial concept studied. Add to a note as understanding develops rather than creating disconnected summaries after every conversation. Preserve useful examples, corrections, unresolved questions, and links to related concepts.

### Suggested structure for each concept note

1. Plain-language meaning
2. Everyday examples
3. What the concept includes—and what it does not
4. Mechanisms and causal relationships
5. Important distinctions and related terminology
6. Measurement and evidence
7. Assumptions, boundaries, and limitations
8. Connections to other concepts and workstreams
9. Practical implications
10. What is established, illustrative, inferred, or unresolved
11. Questions to revisit and change history

This is a flexible guide, not a mandatory checklist. Adapt it to the concept being investigated. Do not force irrelevant sections into a note.

## Documentation discipline

- Explain the real-world phenomenon before relying on technical language.
- Keep everyday usefulness, broad economic production, and the GDP production boundary distinct.
- Distinguish output, revenue, costs, profit, price differences, and value added.
- Label hypothetical numbers and examples as illustrative.
- Do not turn an example into evidence about an actual market or event.
- Record important corrections and uncertainties instead of silently erasing them.
- Link to Workstream B when a concept requires a formal measurement specification; conceptual notes do not replace measurement protocols.
- Consider Workstream E implications when justified, without forcing every concept to produce a commercial idea.
