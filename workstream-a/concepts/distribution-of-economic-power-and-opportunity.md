# Concept 31 — Distribution of Economic Power and Opportunity

**Workstream:** A — Deep Conceptual Foundations
**Status:** Foundational conceptual note; not a quantified inequality assessment or current market diagnosis.
**Date:** 2026-10-30

This note investigates how access, ownership, control, influence, opportunity, benefits, costs and risk are distributed across people, firms, regions and institutions. It distinguishes observed disparities from explanations for them and requires evidence before making claims about causes or misconduct.
