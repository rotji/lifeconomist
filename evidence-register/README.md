# Evidence Register

Record observations and evidence without forcing an immediate state classification.

For each observation preserve:
- Stable record ID
- Research question or claim
- Indicator or mechanism
- Economy/geography
- Observation period
- Publication date
- Source and source identifier
- Value and unit
- Seasonal adjustment and nominal/real basis where applicable
- Evidence quality and limitations
- Fact/interpretation/hypothesis status
- Possible transmission mechanism
- Alternative explanations
- Follow-up action

A spreadsheet may hold the working table, but source documents and definitions must remain traceable. Do not silently overwrite revised values; preserve or record revision history. When a source revises an observation or a new source conflicts with it, retain the vintage/provenance, assess the discrepancy, and review dependent calculations or interpretations rather than replacing them automatically. When resuming monitoring, inspect the last checkpoint and existing evidence before repeating collection. See the [project-wide Continuity, Pause, and Revision Principle](../RESEARCH-METHODOLOGY.md#continuity-pause-and-revision-principle).
